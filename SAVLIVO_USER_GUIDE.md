# Savlivo User Guide

Welcome to Savlivo.

Savlivo helps you understand, manage, and reduce the cost of your subscriptions. It brings your subscriptions, renewals, savings, recommendations, and management tools together in one place.

> **Important:** Savlivo does not silently change your subscriptions. When an action requires a change with a subscription provider, Savlivo asks for your approval and guides you through the required provider step.

---

## 1. Getting started

After signing in, Savlivo opens on your Home screen.

Before relying on totals and recommendations, make sure your subscriptions are entered correctly, including:

- Service
- Subscription plan
- Monthly price
- Billing provider
- Renewal date
- Current status

You can also choose your country and currency in **Settings → Currency & region**.

Savlivo uses this information to calculate spending, savings, upcoming renewals, and recommendations.

---

## 2. Home

Home gives you a quick overview of your subscription finances.

Depending on your subscriptions, you can see information such as:

- Current monthly savings
- Annual savings
- Current monthly subscription spend
- Number of active subscriptions
- Next renewal
- Annual subscription spend
- Items that may need your attention
- Recommended next actions

Tap a card to open the related area when available.

### Understanding savings

Savings represent subscription costs that Savlivo considers avoided based on the status and history of your subscriptions.

Your monthly and annual figures are designed to give you a simple picture of the impact of subscriptions you have paused or cancelled.

---

## 3. Subscriptions

Open **Subscriptions** to see the services currently tracked by Savlivo.

Each subscription can contain information such as:

- Service name
- Plan
- Monthly price
- Billing provider
- Renewal date
- Status
- Savings accumulated so far

### Add a subscription

Tap **+ Add service**.

Choose the service and enter or confirm the subscription information.

Savlivo may provide a local reference price when pricing information is available for your selected country and billing provider.

Always use your actual billed amount if it differs from the suggested price.

### Edit a subscription

Tap the edit button on a subscription card.

You can update information such as its plan, price, billing provider, renewal date, or other available details.

Keeping this information accurate improves Savlivo's calculations and recommendations.

---

## 4. Pause, Cancel and Reactivate

Savlivo can help you manage the status of a subscription.

Depending on the subscription and provider, available actions can include:

- **Pause**
- **Cancel**
- **Reactivate**

### How management actions work

Savlivo does not silently cancel or modify a third-party subscription.

When a provider requires the change to be completed externally, Savlivo can direct you to the appropriate provider management page.

After completing the provider step, return to Savlivo and confirm the resulting status when prompted.

This keeps Savlivo's records aligned with the actual subscription.

### Provider-billed subscriptions

Some subscriptions are managed through another billing platform, such as:

- Apple
- Google Play
- Amazon
- A mobile carrier
- A TV provider

In these cases, Savlivo may direct you to that billing provider rather than the streaming or subscription service itself.

---

## 5. Savings

The **Savings** screen shows the financial impact of subscription changes.

It is intended to answer questions such as:

- How much am I currently saving?
- How much could this save over a year?
- Which subscription changes have contributed to my savings?
- Where might I find additional savings?

Savings figures depend on the subscription information recorded in Savlivo, so accurate prices and statuses are important.

---

## 6. Autopilot

Autopilot is Savlivo's recommendation and decision-support feature.

It analyzes the subscription information available to Savlivo and can highlight opportunities such as:

- Subscriptions worth reviewing
- Potential savings
- Services that may be candidates to pause or cancel
- Subscription-management priorities

Autopilot is designed to help you make decisions — not make unapproved decisions for you.

**Savlivo asks before subscription changes are made.**

Autopilot is available with the supported Premium plan.

---

## 7. Savlivo AI

Savlivo AI lets you ask questions about your subscriptions and get help managing them.

For example, you can ask:

- "How much am I spending each month?"
- "What am I saving?"
- "What renews next?"
- "Which subscription should I review?"
- "What does Autopilot do?"
- "Help me cancel Netflix."
- "Pause YouTube Premium."

Savlivo AI can use the subscription context available in the app to provide more relevant answers.

### Guided actions

If you ask Savlivo AI to help pause, cancel, or reactivate a service, it can guide you toward the appropriate management step.

You remain in control of the final action.

---

## 8. Using voice with Savlivo AI

Savlivo AI supports voice input.

Tap the **microphone button** in the Savlivo AI composer and speak naturally.

When you finish speaking, stop the recording. Savlivo transcribes your speech and sends the resulting request to the assistant.

This is the recommended voice experience because it is integrated directly with Savlivo AI.

Your device keyboard may also provide its own dictation microphone. Keyboard dictation is an operating-system feature and simply converts speech into text inside the text field.

---

## 9. Spoken AI responses

Savlivo can provide spoken playback for supported AI responses.

This can be useful when you prefer to listen rather than read the assistant's answer.

Voice quality and available voices may depend on the device and operating system.

Some advanced functionality, including supported Autopilot and Savlivo AI features, may require Premium.

## 11. Settings

Settings contains account information and app preferences.

### Account & plan

View information such as:

- Account email
- Current Savlivo plan

You can also open plan management from here.

### Preferences

#### Appearance

Switch between:

- Light mode
- Dark mode

Savlivo remembers your selected appearance.

#### Currency & region

Choose your country or region.

Savlivo uses the selected region to determine the appropriate currency and available regional subscription pricing information.

Where verified pricing is available, Savlivo can use it as a reference when adding or reviewing subscriptions.

Your actual billed price should always take priority over a reference price.

### Notifications

Settings includes controls for notification categories such as:

- Renewal reminders
- Savings opportunities

Notification availability can depend on device permissions and the current Savlivo build.

### Premium & Autopilot

Premium controls include settings related to automated recommendations and approval.

Savlivo's core safety principle remains:

**Ask before changes.**

You may also see controls intended to protect services from future automated recommendations or actions.

### Privacy & data

Privacy and account-management functions may include options such as:

- Export data
- Delete account

Features marked **Later** are not yet available in the current version.

## 12. Renewal reminders

Savlivo can use renewal information to help you see upcoming subscription renewals.

For useful reminders, make sure each subscription has an accurate renewal date.

If notifications are enabled, your device may also require permission for Savlivo to display notifications.

## 13. Regional pricing

Subscription prices vary by country, plan, and billing provider.

Savlivo can use regional pricing information to help populate or verify subscription costs.

However, subscription companies can change their prices, and individual accounts may have different pricing.

For that reason:

**Your actual billed amount is the authoritative price for your subscription.**

If Savlivo's suggested price differs from your bill, enter the amount you actually pay.

---

## 14. Understanding subscription status

Savlivo tracks subscription status so spending and savings calculations remain meaningful.

Typical statuses include:

### Active

The subscription is currently active and normally contributes to current subscription spending.

### Paused

The subscription has been paused.

Savlivo can use this state when calculating avoided spending and savings.

### Cancelled

The subscription has been cancelled.

Depending on the provider and billing period, access may continue until the provider's effective cancellation date.

### Reactivated

A paused or cancelled subscription can be returned to active status when appropriate.

Keep Savlivo updated after making changes with the provider.

## 15. Troubleshooting

### Savlivo shows the wrong subscription price

Open the subscription and check:

1. Country and currency
2. Billing provider
3. Subscription plan
4. Monthly price

Enter your actual billed price if it differs from Savlivo's reference pricing.

### My savings look wrong

Check the affected subscription's:

- Monthly price
- Status
- Status effective date
- Renewal information

Savings calculations depend on these values.

### My microphone does not work

Check that Savlivo has microphone permission in your device settings.

Then reopen Savlivo and try the microphone button again.

If the keyboard microphone works but Savlivo's microphone does not, the app's microphone permission or voice-transcription service may need attention.

### Savlivo AI cannot answer

Check your internet connection and try again.

Savlivo AI and voice transcription require access to Savlivo's assistant services.

### I changed a subscription on the provider's website

Return to Savlivo and make sure its status and relevant dates match what happened with the provider.

## 16. Frequently asked questions

### Does Savlivo cancel subscriptions automatically?

No. Savlivo is designed to ask before subscription changes and may guide you to the provider when an external action is required.

### Can Savlivo manage subscriptions billed through Apple or Google Play?

Savlivo can help identify the billing route and guide you to the appropriate subscription-management location. The final provider-side action remains under your control.

### Why does Savlivo need my country?

Subscription pricing and currencies vary by region. Your country helps Savlivo display appropriate currency and regional pricing information.

### Can I override Savlivo's suggested price?

Yes. If your actual billed amount differs, use the amount you actually pay.

### Is Autopilot the same as Savlivo AI?

No.

**Autopilot** focuses on analyzing your subscriptions and surfacing recommendations and opportunities.

**Savlivo AI** is the conversational assistant you can ask questions or use for guided subscription-management help.

They complement each other.

### Can I talk to Savlivo instead of typing?

Yes. Use the microphone button in Savlivo AI to speak your request.

### Does Savlivo work in light and dark mode?

Yes. You can switch appearance from Settings.

## 17. A simple Savlivo routine

For the best results:

1. Keep your subscriptions and prices up to date.
2. Check upcoming renewals.
3. Review your savings.
4. Look at Autopilot recommendations.
5. Ask Savlivo AI when you need help understanding or managing something.
6. Confirm subscription changes after completing any required provider step.

The goal is simple: know what you're paying for, understand what is changing, and make subscription decisions with less effort.
