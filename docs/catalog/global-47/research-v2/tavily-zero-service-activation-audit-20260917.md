## Exact causal reconstruction

`run-decodo-live.mjs` → `manualDiscoveryDependencies` supplies Tavily and the covered-service set → `runLive` completes acquisition/interpretation → `runOnlineDiscoveryStage` maps current candidates through `claimsFor` and `discoveryActivation` → keep nonempty missing fields → remove covered services → `Map(service,target)` → `.slice(0,10)` → `runOpenWebResearch` → query limits → Tavily.

The pure offline reconstruction exactly reproduces all ten persisted target IDs. It finds **554/554targets across61/61zero services have field gaps**. No zero service was rejected as sufficiently evidenced. Existing URLs change the activation reason from `NO_SOURCE_RESEARCH_STATE` to `COMMERCIAL_FIELDS_INSUFFICIENT`; they do not suppress activation. The open-web branch does not pass the legacy `providerStop/hardStop` argument. Read/acquisition restrictions are enforced later.

The Map keeps the **last eligible market** for a service but preserves the service’s **first insertion position**. The inventory happens to put Adobe through Bilibili first. It is not a sufficiency-based or opportunity-ranked choice. One known-source market does not suppress a source-less sibling: both enter the gap pool and only the last market survives deduplication.

## Classification rules and limits of the conclusion

SHOULD_HAVE_TRIGGERED means discovery is a reasonable evidence-gathering step, not a predicted VERIFIED gain or permission to exceed current budgets. Missing sources, empty shells, blocked individual URLs, missing explicit currency/cadence/product relationships and unproven channel roles can justify seeking another admissible source. A403or robots restriction on one source does not prove all public provider/support/qualified-distributor alternatives are unavailable. Nothing here authorizes bypassing those restrictions.

No unsearched service has demonstrated already-sufficient canonical evidence with only a pure conversion defect. No retained record establishes a service-wide prohibition that makes all alternate-source discovery unreasonable. Therefore categoriesB andD are zero. CategoryC is limited to GAİN,Headspace and stc tv: reviewed evidence is special/promotion or annual-only, without an explicit alternative ordinary-monthly branch demonstrated. Their omission is defensible, but the actual code omitted them by rank—not by a noncanonical-evidence gate.

Four services previously labelled ONLY_NONCANONICAL are nevertheless discovery-eligible under this task’s different question:
- **Cinobo**: The retained provider says from 7.49 EUR/month and cancel anytime. That is not an exact canonical plan price, but is a concrete monthly-offer lead: a better pricing/order source could establish named tier and exact amount.

- **Strava**: Retained audit explicitly records a Monthly toggle while selected values are annual equivalents. The toggle is a concrete reason to seek monthly price evidence, not permission to divide the annual bill.
- **Xbox Game Pass**: Retained card says first month $1, then literal $XX.XX/month. The explicit post-intro monthly branch gives a narrow discovery question: obtain the actual regular amount and explicit currency. The intro and placeholder remain noncanonical.

The current prices for these four remain noncanonical. Discovery eligibility does not change that conclusion. This distinction raises discovery-eligible services from the conservative54 to58. Even treating all seven old noncanonical cases as ineligible leaves10/54(18.52%)coverage; the representativeness decision is unchanged.
