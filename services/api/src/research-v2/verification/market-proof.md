# Bounded market-proof continuation

## Audit before implementation

`attribution.mjs:attribute/combinedAttribute` joins explicit offer-owned country
metadata, compound provider declarations (`provider-market.mjs`) and the existing
observed-market rules. Embedded/global catalogs cannot borrow page geo. The
canonical gate consumes one applicability result; task country is not proof.
`fieldsFor` emits `MARKET_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED`. Qualified adjudication can
retain an unlocalized price without claiming the investigation market.

`price-evidence-needs.mjs` already represented a researchable `market` assertion,
and provided a country/availability query and destination bonus. Thus the gap was
not simply an absent market keyword. That compact projection is planning-only,
and did not carry the immutable sources required for independent verification.
`chooseInformationAction` could also stop on sufficient *unlocalized* service
coverage before pursuing the outstanding market fact. Direct navigation exposed
links, while independent Decodo interpretation did not feed market-specific links
back to this planner. `loadLiveSource` and `deriveEvidence` re-opened individual
acquisitions; no identity-bound market join united separate price/market pages.

First missing link: preservation of an otherwise-sufficient **identity-specific
market objective**, followed by `ACQUISITION_RESULT_NOT_JOINED` /
`VERIFIER_CANNOT_BIND`. Other effects: `OBJECTIVE_NOT_PRESERVED`,
`DESTINATION_NOT_DERIVED`, and generic terminal/Human Review states after the
known candidate space is exhausted. Disabled discovery is an expected capability
limit, not permission to invent a destination or market fact. These are code
findings, not a reconstruction of unprovided production acquisition artifacts.

## Evidence taxonomy and scope

Every accepted item requires reviewed first-party authority, current intact
retained bytes, an exact locator and applicable identity scope. Applicability,
attribution and scope are aspects of one market decision, not three independent
vetoes. HIGH/MEDIUM still depends on all other ordinary pricing requirements.

| Class | Accepted boundary / effect |
|---|---|
| Explicit provider statement | New bounded exact named-plan declaration; positive or negative target-country assertion |
| Provider market selector | Existing compound selected-country/page/section patterns only; a selector link alone is a lead |
| Contractual scope | A supported exact named-plan declaration in current provider terms can establish scope; vague service availability cannot |
| Structured market metadata | Existing co-owned country/eligibleRegion/areaServed rules; arbitrary metadata or another product's country cannot bind the offer |
| Localized surface + independent binding | Existing corroborated provider patterns remain; URL/language/currency individually remain hints |
| Cross-source binding | New same-provider, exact-origin, exact-plan declaration on B **linking to the exact acquired price surface A**; no amount repetition required |
| Negative evidence | Exact target-country negative wins over positive evidence for the same plan; conflict is preserved, not voted away |

The new declaration grammar intentionally supports bounded whole assertions
(`Plan is [not] available in Country`, `Plan ist [nicht] in Country verfügbar`)
in visible `p/li/dd` elements, using CLDR country-name vocabulary. It does not
translate arbitrary prose, mine JSON strings, accept quoted examples, hidden
content, or known archived headings. Unrecognized/conditional wording remains
unresolved. Existing supported structured/selector classes are not replaced.
Service-wide evidence does not become a new wildcard for all plans. Cross-host
joins, ambiguous identities, amount-only JSON, and unsupported declarations are
not admitted by this change.

## Continuation and trust boundary

The offline interpreter emits `market-proof-research.json` with identity keys,
established dimensions, status, source journal references and at most 16 lead
URLs. Its `PLANNING_ONLY_REOPEN_SOURCES_FOR_VERIFICATION` meaning is explicit.
It neither sets a verifier field nor grants authority. Source references are
carried in the existing target snapshot into the next ordinary acquisition.

`loadMarketProofSources` re-opens journal records/body hashes and checks service,
target, current reviewed provider, invalidation/quarantine and recorded acquisition
age. The interpreter includes admissible retained price sources in the next
ordinary corpus; the targeted worker independently repeats those checks. No
historical acquisition or interpretation is rewritten. Price facts are derived
again from the original bytes; no saved strong/confidence flag grants evidence.

The market declaration must name the exact independently owned price plan.
For separate sources B must link to A's exact HTTPS URL, and both must have the
same first-party origin and reviewed service/provider identity. Sources must be
at most 30 days old (or the target's stricter retained age), and separated by at
most seven days. Missing recorded capture time fails closed. Price qualifications,
commercial phase, currency and interval remain those of A. An explicit negative
or competing assertion is retained; it cannot be erased by finding another page.
Supporting market hashes and capture times travel into verifier results,
adjudication and retained summaries, preserving invalidation/freshness checks.

Source-pool limits: eight occurrences, eight MB decoded bodies. Objective limit:
16 identities. Lead limit: 16 exact same-origin links, selected from explicit
provider links concerning terms, markets or membership. Bounds fail closed.
No locale siblings are generated and no recursive crawler is added.

## Routing and termination

Direct remains preferred when permitted. With Direct OFF and Decodo ON the same
independent Decodo route/reservation/robots/geo/cost/verifier stack runs; its
existing `DIRECT_PROHIBITED_DECODO_PERMITTED` reason is preserved, while the plan
also records `objective=MARKET_PROOF` and identity keys. Geo transport is never
used as the new cross-source declaration.

Tavily remains discovery-only: the query names the pending plan and requested
country, and search results enter normal provider admission. Research Leads take
that same route. Groq and Browser policies are unchanged and neither is required
or granted authority. Unsupported language or interaction may remain unresolved.

Only market-relevant destinations enter an active market-proof continuation.
The existing completed-resource history applies across Direct and Decodo, with
no refresh/reacquisition exception for this objective. Existing two-search,
per-service transport and monetary limits/reservations remain unchanged. Resume
does not refund anything. All provider requests still pass the ordinary guards.

An unlocalized retained review does not terminate an explicitly pending market
objective. **One sufficient target-market price still stops research**; there is
no exhaustive-plan requirement. A contradicted objective stops, and unavailable
capabilities/no useful destination/budget exhaustion use existing honest terminal
reasons with `MARKET_NOT_VERIFIED`. No positive result is manufactured. Human
Review semantics are unchanged.

## Regression matrix and local proof

The persisted-journal integration tests cover explicit same-source statements,
locale/language/EUR-only negatives, a separate linked provider source, service-only
and unlinked ambiguity, one-of-two-plan applicability, explicit negatives,
contradiction, wrong country, unsupported qualification, stale/invalidation,
annual and commitment-qualified prices, included zero and hidden/archived text.
The real interpreter and targeted worker are invoked offline, not mocked.

Controller tests cover Direct, independent Decodo, Tavily discovery followed by
provider admission, Tavily OFF, both transports OFF, unverified lead admission,
resource suppression across transports, exhausted frozen budget, no actionable
destination, safe zero-network sufficiency, negative terminal state and one-price
sufficiency. Existing Human Review lead and acquisition safeguards remain tested
by the broader suites.

Synthetic proof: source A's two 11.99/21.99 monthly renewal identities initially
have only missing market proof; an objective routes to linked provider terms via
independent Decodo; source B names each plan and country and links back to A;
the ordinary interpreter re-opens A+B and both targeted identities verify.
Changing B to locale/language/currency hints yields no verified identity. Negative
declarations yield `MARKET_CONTRADICTED`. A statement about Basic alone admits
only Basic. This does not claim the production provider has these declarations.

Compatibility identities advance to `V2_FIELD_VERIFICATION_V5` and
`SOURCE_BOUND_OFFER_ELIGIBILITY_V4`, with `SOURCE_BOUND_MARKET_PROOF_V1` on the new
proof. Completed historical jobs remain immutable. Old targets without the new
planning projection retain their old behavior until a normal new interpretation
produces one. No recovery, migration, new transport, or database is introduced.

Services without prices, user-entered price precedence, pricing strategies,
recurring-zero exclusion, authority admission, frozen budgets, Human Review and
Genesis are unchanged. This capability can stop unresolved by design.
