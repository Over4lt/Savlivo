# Source-bound regional commercial market proof

The corroboration path adds scoped proof, not page-wide market inheritance.
`OFFER_MARKET_CORROBORATION_V1` records observations, strengths, dependency roots,
contributing dimensions and the semantic rule that crossed the threshold.

Authority, current intact bytes, exact source/offer binding and compatible final
and canonical context remain admission requirements. The bounded Contentful
adapter accepts either active application locale or active CMS page locale; if
both exist they must agree. The page must be non-preview, regional, commercial,
and own the exact legal/offer leaf and its re-derived named-price spans.

Localization route, application locale and CMS locale share one dependency root.
Repeating them cannot increase strength. A regional commercial surface plus exact
owned offer crosses the threshold when corroborated by a distinct transaction
context: either exact-plan regional purchase language or a provider-owned billing
form. Neither transaction form is mandatory when the other establishes proof.
Existing direct exact-offer structured/country proof remains an independent path.

The billing adapter reads a bounded form: same-origin compatible action, one exact
named plan/product/subscription input, and a billing-country select accepting the
target country. Hidden/template forms, ambiguous subjects, unrelated/support/
locale controls and foreign actions are excluded. A disabled target-country option
is relevant negative evidence for that exact offer, even when purchase prose would
otherwise establish it. No form is submitted or destination acquired.

There is no numeric score or signal-count threshold. Weak/supporting observations
cannot establish market regardless of repetition. Once a semantic threshold is
crossed, missing duplicate locale metadata or an alternative transaction proof is
not a veto. Actual bound restrictions, incompatible scope, unknown qualifications,
and integrity/freshness failures retain their existing effect. Absence of a
restriction is never an observation contributing positive proof.

The proof stores the page/locale/legal locators and purchase span. Neither locale,
currency, dictionary values, feature availability nor sibling catalogs can supply
this proof. Normal price verification still decides authority, currency, cadence,
commercial role and zero-price eligibility. Foreign explicit scopes still conflict
with target applicability and remain isolated from other market identities.

Named scope qualifications are checked in visible bounded assertions and the
owned legal text. Exact-plan headings can bind a short assertion. The deterministic
grammar distinguishes availability, resident/customer location, billing country,
exclusive country sets and exclusions. CLDR country names/codes and a bounded
adjective vocabulary are recognition vocabulary, not service-specific policy.
Unsupported named applicability conditions remain review-only. A negative wins;
unknown qualifications prevent the compound proof becoming unconditional.

Cross-source restrictions retain the existing exact-plan, linked-price-source,
provider/origin and capture-window requirements. Expired sources are excluded by
the existing loader; contradictory admissible sources are not resolved by simply
choosing the newest positive assertion. Historical completed artifacts are not
rewritten. New interpretations re-open sources and re-derive these proofs.

Equal intro and renewal amounts in a single legal leaf are matched using their
complete existing source-bound prose relationship, not path/amount alone, when
transferring derived evidence and when the verifier locates its witness. Repeated
occurrences persist their own relationship spans; the verifier does not apply the
representative occurrence's coordinates to a different locator. Missing or tampered
per-occurrence witnesses cannot borrow a valid sibling relationship.

This is a bounded language/structure implementation, not unrestricted natural
language understanding. It does not execute scripts, authenticate, acquire new
sources or introduce a model dependency.
